## Company-specific thesis to test

SK hynix is not one uniform “AI memory” earnings stream. The value driver is the **mix and cash conversion** of (1) HBM, whose value depends on qualification, yield, advanced packaging, customer breadth and realized margin; (2) server DRAM, whose economics depend on high-capacity modules, demand durability and supply discipline; (3) conventional DRAM, where ASP and inventory remain cyclical; and (4) NAND / Solidigm, where enterprise SSD mix competes with price and supply elasticity. HBM growth creates value only after packaging and yield costs, CAPEX, customer concentration and the opportunity cost of DRAM wafer allocation.

## Peer rule

Micron is the direct company-level PER anchor because it shares DRAM/NAND manufacturing, capital intensity and memory price-cycle exposure. Samsung is a segment reference only because its group earnings include non-memory businesses. Kioxia / SanDisk are NAND-only references; NVIDIA, Broadcom and TSMC are demand / technology context, not earnings-multiple peers. Do not average these groups.

## Release gate
